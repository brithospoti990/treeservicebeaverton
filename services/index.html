Five services, one crew. Each page below explains how the work is done, what Beaverton and Washington County require, and how to tell whether it is the service your tree needs.

What we do

Not sure which one applies? Describe the tree on the phone or in the form and we'll tell you.

Tree removal

For trees that are dead, failing, storm-split or too big for their spot. Most come down in sections on ropes, because few Beaverton yards have room to fell a tree whole. The page covers the warning signs, the method, permits, and what happens to the wood.

Emergency tree service

For a tree on the house, the car, the driveway or the wires, and for cracked limbs hanging over places people use. The page walks through the first hour, who handles power lines, and what your insurer will ask for.

Emergency tree service in Beaverton

Tree trimming and maintenance

Deadwood, clearance over roofs and streets, weight reduction before ice season, young-tree structure, fruit trees and hedges. The page shows where a proper cut goes, what each season is good for, and the city's clearance rules.

Tree trimming in Beaverton

Stump grinding

The stump is shaved down below the lawn so you can topsoil and seed. The page explains grind depths, the 811 utility locate, what to do with the chips, and which stumps sprout again.

Stump grinding in Beaverton

Stump removal

The whole stump and its main roots dug out, and the hole backfilled and compacted. It is the right choice under patios, footings, fence posts and driveways, and the page has a chooser that tells you which one your project needs.

Stump removal in Beaverton

What is the same on every job

  • A site walk and a written scope: which trees and limbs, how they come down, where the wood and chips go, whether the stump is included.
  • A check of the tree's status before booking. Street trees need a city permit to remove, some backyard trees are protected, and much of Aloha, Cedar Mill and Bethany follows county rules. The permit checker gives a first answer.
  • An 811 utility locate before any stump work.
  • Cleanup, and a walkthrough with you before the crew leaves.
