# Design notes (for the pages still to come)

**Idea.** The site looks at a yard the way an arborist does: a plan-view sketch of the lot with numbered tree tags. The tag (clipped top corners, punched hole) is the logo, the favicon and the marker on the hero drawing. That drawing is the one loud element. Everything else stays quiet.

**Colour.** vellum `#EEF2EC` page, paper `#FAFCF8` surfaces, fir `#12382C` headings and dark bands, canopy `#2F6B4F` numerals, link `#1F5C44`, wash `#D5E4D8`, line `#C3D1C6`, ink `#1B2622`, muted `#4A5B53`. Hi-vis `#D9F24B` is for actions only (buttons, tags, the phone number on dark). Signal `#C73E1D` is for emergencies only.

**Type.** Barlow Condensed 600 for H1, H2 and big numerals; Barlow 400/500/600 for everything else. Sentence case. No all-caps labels, no arrows on links, no eyebrow labels above headings.

**Layout.** Left-aligned. Sections use the "ledger": H2 in a sticky left column, content on the right, stacked on phones. Rows and rules instead of card grids. Dark fir bands for the local-conditions block and the estimate form.

**Buttons.** Hi-vis fill, fir border, solid offset shadow that closes on hover. Ghost button for the second action.

**Rules carried to every page.** No prices. No claims that are not verified. American spelling. Phone numbers never wrap. FAQ answers 300 characters or fewer. One business schema node (`/#business`) referenced everywhere, never a second LocalBusiness.
