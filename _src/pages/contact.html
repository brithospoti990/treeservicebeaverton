What helps when you call

  • The address, and whether it is inside Beaverton or in unincorporated county, if you know. It decides which tree rules apply.
  • What the tree is doing: dead, leaning, dropping limbs, too close to the house, or simply overdue for pruning.
  • What is underneath it: a roof, a fence, wires, a neighbor's yard.
  • How we get to it: gate width, slopes, and whether the back yard has vehicle access.
  • A couple of phone photos, one of the whole tree and one of the base. Send them after the call and we can often tell which equipment the job needs before we arrive.

Estimates are free and in writing. They happen at your property, because nobody can assess a tree from a desk. The office address above is where mail and paperwork go.
