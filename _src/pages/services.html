What is the same on every job

  • A site walk and a written scope: which trees and limbs, how they come down, where the wood and chips go, whether the stump is included.
  • A check of the tree's status before booking. Street trees need a city permit to remove, some backyard trees are protected, and much of Aloha, Cedar Mill and Bethany follows county rules. The permit checker gives a first answer.
  • An 811 utility locate before any stump work.
  • Cleanup, and a walkthrough with you before the crew leaves.
